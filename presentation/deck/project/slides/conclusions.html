Conclusions

Sustainable substitution is real, but conditional

D2

Normalisation compresses but never reverses the gap between ruminant meat and pulses; per calorie, nutrition and climate conflict.

D3

Green swaps are rarely nutritionally equivalent (4.5 %): protein, energy, B12 and zinc are the binding nutrients.

D4

Baskets can cut climate impact 38–82 % within explicit nutrient bounds — at a price in similarity, change and sometimes water.

D5

6 of 9 headline claims survive every pre-declared test; the other 3 hold under one stated condition.

All of it rests on D1: 2,272 foods joined exactly, with every gap, duplicate and data error accounted for.
