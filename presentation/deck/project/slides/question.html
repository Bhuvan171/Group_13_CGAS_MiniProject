Research question

Which food substitutions genuinely reduce environmental impact while preserving nutritional value — and where do unavoidable trade-offs remain once nutritional equivalence is enforced?

Footprints ignore nutrition

Life-cycle rankings compare foods per kilogram, as if 100 g of lettuce and 100 g of beef did the same job.

Nutrition ignores footprints

Nutrient profiling says what a food delivers, never what it costs the planet to produce.

Our framing

Treat substitution as a constrained, multi-objective problem: compare like with like, enforce nutrition, report trade-offs.
